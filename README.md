## 🚀 What Is This?

This is a **5lbs Drum Spinner Combat Robot** designed entirely in Autodesk Fusion 360.

The robot features a horizontal drum-style spinning weapon mounted at the front of the chassis. It uses two Dartbox V2 drive units with Banebots Colson wheels for movement and a FingerTech Beater Bar as its primary weapon.

The main idea behind this design was to create a compact combat robot with a simple drive layout and a powerful front-mounted spinning weapon.

The entire robot was modelled in Fusion 360, including the chassis, motor mounts, drive system, weapon arrangement, and component layout.

## 💡 Why I Made It

I made this project to improve my CAD skills and gain practical knowledge of mechanical design.

Instead of just modelling individual parts, I wanted to design an entire combat robot where every component had to fit and work together.

While designing it, I worked on:

- Chassis and structural frame design.
- Motor mounting and drive system arrangement.
- Wheel and hub positioning.
- Weapon mounting and belt-drive layout.
- Electronics placement and internal space management.
- Solving mechanical clearance and component-fitting problems.

Honestly, it was a great learning experience. I got to understand how different mechanical systems come together to create one complete machine.

**Learning by doing! :)**

## 🔩 Assembly Guide

  <img src="https://media.printables.com/media/prints/1895785/rich_content/73d4afba-53c4-43cd-964e-b55e909b0e2c/roboxdrumspinner.png" alt="Combat Robot Assembly View" width="800">
</p>

Follow these steps to assemble the robot.

**Step 1: Print the Chassis**

3D-print the chassis, top plate, and other required printed components. Clean the parts and remove any unwanted printing material.

**Step 2: Install the Motor Mounts**

Attach the two Dartbox aluminium motor mounts to the designated positions on the chassis walls. Make sure both mounts are properly aligned.

**Step 3: Install the Drive Motors**

Mount the two Dartbox V2 drive units onto their respective motor mounts. Secure them properly.

**Step 4: Attach the Wheels**

Install the wheel hubs onto the motor shafts and attach the Banebots Colson wheels. Check that both wheels are properly secured and rotate freely.

**Step 5: Install the Weapon System**

Mount the FingerTech Beater Bar at the front of the chassis. Install the weapon motor and belt-drive arrangement according to the CAD design.

Check the weapon assembly for proper alignment and mechanical clearance.

**Step 6: Install the Electronics**

Install the ESC, receiver, battery, and required wiring inside the chassis. Keep all wires secured and away from moving mechanical components.

**Step 7: Attach the Cover Plates**

Once the mechanical and electrical components are installed, attach the top and other cover plates.

**Step 8: Final Inspection**

Before powering the robot, check all fasteners, motor mounts, wheel hubs, wiring, and mechanical clearances.

> ⚠️ **Safety First:** Never test a spinning weapon without proper guarding and a controlled test environment. Keep the robot immobilized during initial motor tests, use a weapon lock during handling, and follow the applicable combat robotics safety rules.
